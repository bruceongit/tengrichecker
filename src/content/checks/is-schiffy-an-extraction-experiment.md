## Our response: we are building for the long term

**Team statement: this accusation is false.** SCHIFFY does not exist to extract money from its community. We are backing all our NFTs with Robinhood stock tokens. That backing comes at a cost to our margins, and we are choosing to bear that cost as part of a long-term approach.

Calling that an “extraction experiment” ignores the commitment we are making. We are committing resources to NFT backing and accepting lower margins. Judge us on what we build and deliver. A separate trading fleet is not proof of our motives.

The NFT backing and margin statements above are supplied by the team. This trading review has not audited the backing or measured its cost.

“Robinhood stock tokens” means the tokenized instruments, not direct ownership of company shares. [Robinhood explains the distinction here](https://robinhood.com/rhj/stocktokens/). That source describes Robinhood’s product; it does not verify our NFT backing.

## His accusation is not established by the trades

He is wrong to treat the fleet's trades as proof of an extraction scheme run by our team. A claim about motives cannot be settled by pointing to wallets he has not linked to us.

## Evidence against the framing

- **$SCHIFFY is a small share of received transfer events.** It is about **0.27%** of the token transfers these wallets have received since Aug 29, across 2,542 tokens. This measures event count, not trading value or profit.
- **The same entry appears on at least 1,400 other launches**, from 1,150 different deployers. 431 of those came before $SCHIFFY existed.
- **On 1,076 of them** the fleet also took at least 1% of supply, sold at least 90% of what it bought, and had a lockstep sell.
- **The entry wasn't unusually early.** The fleet's median entry on those launches is 65 seconds after mint. On $SCHIFFY it was 263 seconds, slower than on 1,184 of the 1,400. That doesn't look like a head start.

## The unusual hold still needs an explanation

- **The hold was rare.** On 840 of those 1,076 launches, the fleet had fully sold within about an hour. On $SCHIFFY it held 8.76% of supply through a sell-free gap of about 13.6 days.
- **Few others match at that scale.** Only BOP and COMPUTER had gaps of 14 days or more at 5%+ of supply. Nine launches reach 14 days at 1%+ of supply.
- **The timing lines up with the alleged promotion dates.** The gap covers the Sept 3–12 push the thread describes, and selling resumed Sept 13. Those promotion dates have not been independently verified.

These numbers can't tell us why the fleet held. A trader might hold a token that keeps rising, or might know something. We haven't tested which.

## Limits of this ruling

Durations are estimated from block numbers at about 9.9 blocks per second. Our launch count is probably low, because it only includes blocks where at least 6 of these 16 wallets bought together.
