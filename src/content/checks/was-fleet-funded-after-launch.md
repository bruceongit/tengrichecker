## What the record shows

$SCHIFFY was minted on **Aug 29, 2026 at 18:24:23 UTC**. The thread's own funding evidence is dated **Aug 13**, 16 days earlier.

The 16 launch buyers received their first tokens well before launch:

| Wallets | First token received |
|---|---|
| 10 of the 16 | Jul 27, 20:16 UTC |
| 6 of the 16 | Aug 13, 15:02 UTC |

On Jul 27, ten of them bought [DAHOOD](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/tx/0x71cc69017176107c00ab26ed4717a3acf6f02e8998f148dcb43b1733a4d8b097) together, 70 seconds after its mint.

## Why it matters

The order of events carries the accusation. Funding that follows a launch suggests a fleet built for that launch. Funding that comes before it, for wallets already trading other tokens, suggests a fleet that was already running.

## Limits of this ruling

We rated the sequence as the thread states it. We did not test whether these wallets received further top-ups after launch, so we are not saying they never did.
