## What Tengri has shown

Tengri says the wallet kept receiving RON and showed "activity" after the hack. Receiving RON does not need a key, and the [later activity was not signed by the wallet](#claim-jihoz-wallet-actively-used). Nothing Tengri cites links Jihoz to the attacker wallet or to the Tornado Cash deposits.

## What others say

- **zeroShadow** said it found evidence the private key was compromised, that stolen funds went through Tornado Cash, and that later activity "should not be attributed to Jihoz". We have not seen its working. Jihoz describes zeroShadow as Chainalysis and says Jihoz is in touch with Chainalysis about recovery, so we do not treat it as independent of Jihoz.
- **Jihoz** says the hack came from a home invasion and that the details went to the FBI. We have not seen those records and cannot verify them.
- **Tengri** says this rewrites the story, since 2024 reports say PeckShield raised the alert. Cointelegraph's report does credit PeckShield with the public alert. A public alert says nothing about how a key was lost, so this does not settle the question either way.

## Facts that cut the other way

- **The RON is still there.** 51,578.89 RON has built up in a wallet whose key was reportedly stolen, and nobody has taken it. It is worth about $2,540 today, but the 9,851 RON there in January 2025 was worth about $19,000. The attacker wallet has been idle since February 23, 2024. We do not know why the attacker never came back. Whoever holds the key has left this RON alone, and that includes Jihoz if Jihoz still holds it.
- **The rescue used the same key.** See [the wallet-use claim](#claim-jihoz-wallet-actively-used). A key can be held by more than one person, so this does not show who took the funds, but it is a fact any full account has to explain.

## Limits of this ruling

We are not ruling that the hack was genuine. We are ruling that Tengri has not shown it was faked. The Ronin records show the theft and the rescue. They cannot show who was holding the key. We followed the funds on Ronin as far as the bridge and hop wallets, and rely on PeckShield, Beosin and zeroShadow for the Tornado Cash step on Ethereum.

Not reviewed here: Tengri's claims about a $66 price target, about Sky Mavis payout contracts and wallet `0x7c137107…`, and a 20% figure for fake robbery reports. We take no position on those. The separate claim that Sky Mavis planned SCHIFFY is not ruled on in this case. Our response on SCHIFFY is in [the SCHIFFY case](/case/schiffy-fleet).
