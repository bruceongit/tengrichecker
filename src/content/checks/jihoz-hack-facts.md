## What we found

The attacker wallet received its first stolen funds on February 22, 2024 at 23:12 UTC, already February 23 in Asia, which is why reports use both dates. It took about 3.2M RON and other tokens from `0x121ad060…` and `0xa09a9b6f…`, and swapped them for 3,248.87 WETH.

Cointelegraph and PeckShield put the loss at about $9.7M, so "$10M" is a fair round number.

## The small error

RON traded around $2.89 at the time, about 35% below the $4.45 all-time high that CoinGecko records for March 25, 2024. It was a strong run-up, not the peak.
